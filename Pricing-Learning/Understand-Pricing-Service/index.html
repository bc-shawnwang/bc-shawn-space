A. What questions does the Pricing service answer?

The service registers six gRPC services (Boot.scala). Grouped by the question each one answers:

QuestionRPCWhat comes back
What should this shopper see as the price of these products?TaxPricingPrototype.ListEstimatedTaxProductPricesPer product or variant: calculated price, base price, sale, retail, MAP, ranges, bulk tiers, saved, each with tax inc and ex. The rest of this page is about this RPC.
Same, without tax (deprecated)Pricing.ListProductPricesThe same prices before the tax step
Which price list, or discounts, applies to this customer group and channel?Pricing.FindPriceListAssignment, Pricing.FindAlternativePricingThe price list ID and whether prices are entered inc or ex tax, or "discounts", or "none"
What does each cart line cost?CartPricing.GetCartPricingPer cart item: one selected-variant price, with the bulk tier that applies to the cart's quantities
What are the tax inc and ex amounts for these prices?Tax.CalculateTaxPricesInc and ex amount per price line. The estimate RPC calls this in process.
What price lists, records and assignments exist? (manage)PriceLists.*Create, read, update and delete price lists, price records (with bulk tiers) and assignments
What discounts exist, and what would this price be after them?Discounts.*, ApplyCustomerGroupDiscounts, ValidatePriceListAndDiscountStateManage product, category and sitewide discounts per customer group. Apply them to given prices. Check price list and discount settings are consistent.

In short: the first three rows are read paths that answer "what price", the last two are the data behind them. I did not check which clients call GetCartPricing, or whether it is live. The Client Side page covers the clients I did trace.

B. What decides a product's price?

Every factor below can change the price the service returns. They are listed roughly in the order they apply.

FactorHow it affects the price
Catalog pricesThe starting point. Base price, sale price, retail price, MAP, and per-variant values. The "calculated price" (what the shopper pays before the other factors) comes from catalog.
ChannelCatalog is asked for prices in this channel. A price list can be assigned to a channel.
Customer groupSelects the price list assignment and the customer group discounts. Group 0 means everyone.
Price listIf one is assigned, its records replace each variant's catalog prices. A group's own price list beats a channel-wide one. Lists can be layered, with a fallback mode (priority or lowest price) when several lists have a record.
Customer group discountsLower (or with markup on, raise) the calculated price, by percent, amount, or fixed price. Product beats category beats sitewide. Only apply when no price list applies.
Selected variant and optionsDecides which variant's price is used. When options are not fully chosen, the result is a price range or the base product price, depending on store settings.
Option set rules and product rulesAdjust the calculated price by percent, relative amount, or fixed price.
PicklistsThe price of each chosen picklist product is added on top.
Bulk tiers (quantity)A tier lowers the unit price once the quantity reaches its minimum. The product estimate returns tiers as data. The cart applies the matching one.
CurrencyCatalog values are multiplied by the exchange rate. Price list records are stored per currency and looked up by it. Results are rounded to the currency's decimals.
TaxDoes not change the entered price. Adds the inc and ex amounts, using the tax class, the shopper's tax zone, and whether prices are entered inc or ex (price list setting or the store's global one).
Store settings and flagsDecide which logic runs: price lists on or off, discounts on or off, "use lowest variant price" for listings, partial selection pricing, apply bulk tiers after discounts (cart), inc/ex price lists, markup, cascading price lists, and catalog caching.

resetPriceToBaseCatalogPrice is read from store settings, but in the code I read it is only logged, so I left it out.

C. How catalog price, price list, discounts and cart price relate

The order that decides a price. Loops every 8s.

AlwaysCatalog pricethe base, per product and variant
One ofPrice listreplaces the variant's prices
orDiscountslower the calculated price
AlwaysRules + picklistsoption set, product, picklist
Cart onlyBulk tierchosen by cart quantity
Estimate onlyTaxinc and ex amounts
  • Catalog price is the base. It is fetched for every request. Price lists and discounts both start from it.
  • Price list and discounts are alternatives. The service picks one mode per request: price list, discounts, or neither. They never apply together. A customer group's own price list wins, then the group's discounts, then a channel-wide price list.
  • A price list replaces, discounts adjust. A price list record replaces the variant's whole catalog price, per variant. A variant with no record keeps its catalog price. A discount only lowers the calculated price.
  • The cart uses the same inputs for one line item. Same catalog data, same price list or discounts decision, same rules and picklists. It differs in three ways: it prices only the selected variant, never returns ranges, and applies a bulk tier chosen from the quantities in the cart (per variant for price list records, per product for catalog tiers). It has no tax step in this service.

Order of adjustments, estimate vs cart

Price sourceProduct estimateCart price
Price listRules, then picklists. Bulk tiers returned, not applied.Bulk tier first, then rules, then picklists. No customer group discounts.
CatalogRules, then discount, then picklists. Bulk tiers returned, not applied.Rules, then bulk tier and discount (the bulk tier goes after the discount when the "apply bulk tiers after discounts" setting is on, before it otherwise), then picklists.

Catalog bulk tiers never take a price below zero. Read from CartPricingService and OverviewPricingService, not run.
