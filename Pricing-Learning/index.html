Terminology and vocabulary

Grouped by where the word shows up. Price field names match the gRPC ProductEstimatedTaxPricesProto message unless noted.

The service
Pricing service
A Scala/gRPC service (repo pricing) that hosts several RPC APIs: Price Lists (live in production), Pricing (overview/storefront price resolution, discounts, currency projection; in development) and Tax. Per its AGENTS.md.
RPCs it registers
Tax, PriceLists, Pricing, Discounts, TaxPricingPrototype, CartPricing (the register(...) calls in Boot.scala).
TaxPricingPrototype
The RPC storefront and api-proxy call for product prices with tax applied: ListEstimatedTaxProductPrices. "Prototype" because the proto says its eventual home is the tax service.
Overview pricing
The step that builds storefront-style prices (calculated price, ranges, bulk tiers) before tax is applied. OverviewPricingService, called from PricingService.buildPrices.
PricingContext
The per-request inputs to a price: store, customer group, channel, currency, exchange rate, decimal places, country and customer.
Price lists
Price list
A named set of prices managed by the Price Lists RPC, alongside price records, layers and assignments (the four things AGENTS.md lists).
Price record
One price list entry. It carries price, salePrice, retailPrice, mapPrice, an optional calculatedPrice and bulk pricing tiers.
Assignment
Ties a price list to who gets it. The service validates this against its customer-groups and channels clients, so the targets are customer groups and channels.
Alternative pricing
What FindAlternativePricing answers: whether a customer group and channel gets an alternative price (for example a price list) rather than catalog prices. Storefront asks this before keeping the price filter on a facet search.
Price fields
calculated_price
The final per-unit selling price. On a price record it is the record's calculatedPrice, else its salePrice, else its price. Rules, picklists and customer-group discounts are then applied. This becomes price.with_tax / without_tax on Stencil.
price (base)
The regular price before any sale. GraphQL calls it basePrice; Stencil shows it as non_sale_price_*.
sale_price
The sale price, if one applies.
retail_price (RRP)
The retail / recommended price. Stencil: rrp_*.
minimum_advertised_price (MAP)
Minimum advertised price. Stencil: map_price_*.
saved
Retail minus calculated, only when retail is greater. Computed per tax side after tax is applied.
Price range
Lowest and highest variant calculated price for a product (price_range), and the same for retail (retail_price_range).
Bulk tiers
Quantity-based pricing: fixed price, amount off, or percent off, from a minimum quantity up to an optional maximum.
Tax
Tax inclusive / exclusive
Every price comes back in three forms: as_entered, tax_inclusive and tax_exclusive. The inclusive side becomes with_tax, the exclusive side without_tax.
Tax entered as
ENTERED_INCLUSIVE or ENTERED_EXCLUSIVE: whether the merchant keyed prices with tax. It says which side as_entered equals.
TaxDisplay (Inc / Exc / Both)
Storefront's setting for which tax side to show. It is applied on the storefront side, after the price comes back. There are separate list and detail settings.
Tax label
The text next to a price (for example "GST"). It comes from store config, not from pricing.
Products and selections
Variant
One purchasable combination of option values with its own SKU. A price request item can carry a variant_id, option values, both, or neither.
Partial selection
Only some options chosen. The price is then not for a single variant, and the range collapses accordingly.
Currency projection
The service's cached copy of a store's currency settings (exchange rate, decimal places). Used when a request has no exchange rate.
Client side
Storefront service
The Scala service (repo storefront) that renders Stencil page data and serves Storefront GraphQL.
StorefrontGraphQL
The GraphQL API in that service. Its Product.prices field is what reaches the Pricing service.
Stencil / page object
Stencil is the theme engine. The storefront service builds "page objects", the JSON context a template reads, such as category.products.*.price or product.price.
PLP / PDP
Product list page (category, brand, search) and product detail page.
bcapp
The PHP monolith. It is the older implementation of storefront pricing and still serves several paths.
BCData product_attributes
A script object rendered into the PDP head. The theme reads it for the first paint of price and stock state.
Experiment mode
A LaunchDarkly string variation that switches a path between implementations: disable, compare, compare_serve_pricing (traffic switching only) or enable. Anything unrecognised means disable.

Pricing service dependencies (downstream)

What the Pricing service calls or reads. Taken from how Boot.scala wires the service. Per-call behaviour belongs on the Pricing Service page, which is WIP.

DependencyUsed forWired as
StoreDB (per-store MySQL)Price lists, price records, layers, assignments, sitewide discountsStoreDbPriceRecordRepository, StoreDbPricingSitewideDiscountRepository, behind a workload-manager gate
Catalog (gRPC)Product and variant catalog prices, options, categoriesCatalogServiceClient, wrapped by CachingCatalogServiceClient; CartCatalogServiceClient for cart. Can read from the read-only DB.
Currency service (gRPC)Exchange rate and decimal placesCurrencyServiceClient via ProjectingCurrencyProvider, cached in Redis with a TTL
Inventory (gRPC)Shipping origin addresses, for tax zone determinationInventoryServiceClient via ShippingOriginProjectionService
Store config (gRPC)Store settings, including basic tax settingsStoreConfigServiceClient, CachingStoreConfigClient, TaxStoreConfigServiceClient
Customer groups (gRPC)Validating customer groups on price list assignmentsCustomerGroupValidationServiceClient
Channels (gRPC)Validating channels on price list assignmentsChannelsServiceClient
Workload manager (gRPC)Gating DB access and cross-service callsWorkloadManagerClient("pricing")
Redis (Lettuce) and CaffeineTwo-level caches, including the catalog price data cachelettuceCacheBuilder, cache name catalog-price-data
RabbitMQPublishing domain eventsVia the notification service passed into the price list services. Per AGENTS.md; I did not read the publisher.
LaunchDarklyFeature flags across pricing and taxLDClientBuilder.build
Tax (in-process)Applying tax to pricesTaxApplicationService takes the TaxImpl instance directly, so this is a method call inside the same service, not a network hop

bcapp calls the Pricing service differently. For POST /remote/v1/product-attributes it calls FindPriceListAssignment and the Price Lists RPC for data, and does the price calculation and tax itself in PHP. It does not call ListEstimatedTaxProductPrices. Storefront also has a second pricing client that calls bcapp at /internalapi/v1/pricing/products, selected by flag. Details are in section 2 of the Client Side page.
